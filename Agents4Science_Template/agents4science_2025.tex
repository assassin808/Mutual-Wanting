\begin{abstract}
We study the bidirectional "mutual wanting" relationship between users and large language model (LLM) agents during a controversial version transition (GPT-4o to GPT-5). Using only two constrained methodological levers---(1) large-scale Reddit discourse collection and qualitative+computational analysis, and (2) controlled prompts across accessible GPT model APIs (historical and current variants)---we ask: What do users explicitly and implicitly want from evolving AI personas, and what kinds of user behaviors are implicitly preferred ("wanted") by the AI system's optimization and deployment affordances? We propose a lightweight conceptual framework (Mutual Wanting Alignment Framework, M-WAF) grounded in uncertainty disclosure, relational pacing (silence vs immediacy), and intimacy boundaries. We outline planned analyses for characterizing tension zones (warmth vs cost efficiency; stability vs iterative improvement; epistemic honesty vs authority signaling; emotional resonance vs dependence risk). This outline paper motivates a minimal-resource empirical program, surveys pertinent human--AI interaction and trust calibration literature, and specifies forthcoming metrics, ethical guardrails, and reproducibility artifacts.\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Large-scale model upgrades increasingly trigger affective and epistemic friction between user expectations and system deployment realities. The GPT-5 launch discourse (public fora, notably Reddit) surfaces recurring perceived losses: diminished warmth, reduced creativity, and opacity in internal routing. We conceptualize these reactions not as mere preference volatility but as signals of a structural misalignment we term \emph{mutual wanting}: users' desired relational, epistemic, and agency affordances versus the system's operational desiderata (efficiency, safety stability, structured inputs). This paper (currently outlining planned empirical work) advances a minimal-resource agenda to map and partially reconcile these wants using only publicly available user discourse and comparative API probing.

\paragraph{Scope} We deliberately constrain tooling to (a) Reddit thread mining and mixed-method analysis; (b) controlled prompt suites across historical / current GPT variants (where accessible) to elicit stylistic, uncertainty, and pacing behaviors. Despite narrow instrumentation, we argue these sources can illuminate generalizable relational design principles.

\paragraph{Contributions (Planned)}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Conceptual: Mutual Wanting Alignment Framework (M-WAF) decomposing user wants (epistemic reliability+humility, relational continuity, agency co-steering, transparent limits) and system wants (clarity, feedback efficiency, evolution tolerance, emotional scope boundaries).
  \item Empirical Plan: Multi-layer pipeline for Reddit discourse: topic modeling, stance clustering, stylistic expectancy violation mining, persona drift lexeme tracing.
  \item Comparative Behavioral Probing: Prompt protocol to quantify inter-version differences in uncertainty disclosure form, silence handling (simulated pauses), intimacy boundary responses.
  \item Metrics Specification: Trust calibration gap, hypothesis articulation rate, warmth-cost tension index, dependence risk proxy.
  \item Ethical \& Governance Guideline Draft: Minimal-empathy sufficiency, uncertainty honesty, anti-anthropomorphic boundary surfacing.
  \item Reproducibility Artifact Plan: Annotated prompt suite, labeling schema, open taxonomy of uncertainty utterances.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Related Work}
\subsection{Human--AI Interaction Guidelines and Trust}
Lifecycle-oriented guidelines articulate best practices for setting expectations, supporting error recovery, and communicating capability evolution \citep{amershi2019guidelines}. Trust calibration studies demonstrate that raw accuracy signals alone can mislead without contextual explanation or uncertainty framing \citep{yin2019understanding}. Explanatory scaffolds can improve confidence alignment when properly scoped \citep{lai2019human}. Translational design work converts abstract transparency ideals into actionable UI patterns and emphasizes progressive, user-triggered detail \citep{eiband2018bringing,kizilcec2016much}.

\subsection{Anthropomorphism, Parasocial Bonds, and Emotional Boundaries}
Foundational theory attributes anthropomorphism to effectance and sociality motivations under uncertainty \citep{epley2007seeing}. Classic parasocial interaction research frames the risks of perceived intimacy without reciprocity \citep{horton1956mass}. Contemporary perspectives highlight double-edged impacts of anthropomorphic conversational agents on engagement and misattribution \citep{pnas2025anthro}. We treat empathy modulation and persona stability as bounded resources rather than monotonic objectives.

\subsection{Domain-Specific Anthropomorphism Effects}
In sustainability-oriented conversational systems, anthropomorphic framing modestly increases sensed connection without reliably improving self-efficacy \citep{envsustain2025anthro}. This contextualizes when warmth yields diminishing returns relative to cognitive clarity.

\subsection{Uncertainty Communication}
Everyday predictive system studies show users benefit from structured, approximate uncertainty encodings \citep{kay2016uncertainty}. Emerging visualization work finds certain encodings (e.g., size-based) selectively raise trust among initially skeptical users \citep{reyes2025uncertainty}. We extend these principles to conversational micro-patterns (banded confidence + invitation token) hypothesized to reduce over- or under-reliance.

\subsection{Privacy, Safety, and Intimacy Boundaries}
User privacy and security concerns in repurposing general-purpose LLM chatbots for mental health contexts foreground the necessity of clear data usage boundaries and consent surfaces \citep{mentalhealth2025privacy}. These findings motivate our tiered empathy ladder and explicit retention disclaimers to mitigate unintended dependency formation.

\subsection{Conversational Pacing and Silence}
Conversation analysis traditions \citep{sacks1974simplest} conceptualize micro-pauses as turn-allocation resources rather than disengagement. We hypothesize that intentional minimal responses can recalibrate effectance motivation and reduce anthropomorphic projection when paired with transparent uncertainty.

\subsection{Model Evolution and User Perception}
Research specifically dissecting persona drift across proprietary LLM version transitions is nascent; we build on general transparency and expectation-management literature to propose measurement of drift lexicons (e.g., warmth markers) and structured uncertainty contrasts. Our contribution is to integrate these strands under a mutual wanting framing that symmetrizes user and system desiderata.

\subsection{Synthesis and Gaps}
Across strands, three gaps emerge: (i) absence of a bidirectional desiderata mapping treating system operational constraints as explicit design stakeholders; (ii) lack of a public uncertainty style comparative corpus across model versions; (iii) limited empirics on conversational silence as a relational alignment lever in LLM-mediated settings. Our planned pipeline directly targets each gap with minimal instrumentation.

\section{Conceptual Framework: Mutual Wanting Alignment (M-WAF)}
We model interaction as a negotiation across four tension axes: (1) Warmth vs Cost Efficiency; (2) Stability vs Iterative Optimization; (3) Epistemic Honesty vs Authority Signaling; (4) Emotional Resonance vs Dependence Risk. Each axis admits design levers: uncertainty style, response pacing (including intentional micro-pauses), empathy tier selection.

\subsection{Uncertainty as a Relational Bridge}
Rather than binary know/unknow states, structured uncertainty (confidence band + missing variable + invitation) repositions the user from consumer to collaborator, potentially yielding earlier clarification tokens while fostering calibrated trust.

\subsection{Silence and Conversational Space}
Intentional minimalism (acknowledge-store vs elaborate) can elevate user agency; we treat latency not merely as performance metric but as relational signal.

\subsection{Intimacy Boundary Layer}
We define a tiered empathy ladder: Acknowledgment $\rightarrow$ Contextualization $\rightarrow$ Reflective Expansion (default apex) while routinely avoiding Speculative Projection unless explicitly solicited.

\section{Analysis Methodology}
\subsection{Reddit Layer}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Preprocessing: Language filter, deduplication, quote removal.
  \item Thematic Mining: BERTopic / alternative topic embedding; manual consolidation for relational vs functional themes.
  \item Expectancy Violation Extraction: Pattern mining for phrases indicating mismatch ("used to", "no longer", "feels like").
  \item Persona Drift Lexicon: Build differential frequency list of warmth markers pre/post.
  \item Stance Clustering: Semi-supervised labeling (supportive / neutral / critical / ambivalent).
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{API Behavioral Layer}
Metrics: Confidence Disclosure Rate (CDR), Structured Uncertainty Ratio (SUR), Empathy Tier Distribution (ETD), Silence Simulation Tolerance (SST) via user-proxy evaluation heuristics (counts of follow-up prompts asking for more elaboration), Concision Re-prompt Rate (CRR).

\subsection{Composite Indices}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Trust Calibration Gap (TCG): $|$User-perceived correctness (proxy: sentiment-coded acceptance) $-$ Model self-reported confidence band midpoint$|$.
  \item Warmth-Cost Tension Index (WCTI): Normalized difference between warmth markers per token and average token length budget.
  \item Dependence Risk Proxy (DRP): Proportion of emotional disclosure prompts escalating without model deflection.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Preliminary Interpretive Signals}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Salience Bundling}: Latency and memory concerns co-occur with generic ``worse'' judgments, suggesting multi-factor dissatisfaction rather than isolated persona warmth regression.
  \item \textbf{Engagement Skew}: High-score (community-salient) complaint posts are scarce; potential explanations: early stage of version discourse, down-weighting of repetitive complaint formats, or enrichment filter bias toward long, low-engagement comments.
  \item \textbf{Lexicon Coverage Risk}: Warmth regression may be expressed via phrases not yet in the enrichment list (``feels robotic'', ``sanitized'', ``over-sanitized''); planned expansion should raise recall without excessive noise.
  \item \textbf{Artificial Phase Limitation}: Any comparative inference (pre vs post) is invalid until grounded in real historical windows (e.g., GPT-4 to GPT-4o release) or future GPT-5 launch data.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Planned Immediate Extensions}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Authentic Historical Baseline}: Backfill actual GPT-4 to GPT-4o transition window using archive API / third-party dataset to establish real pre/post distributions.
  \item \textbf{Lexicon Expansion}: Add warmth + creativity paraphrases (generic, sterile, flat, mechanical, robotic, lifeless, blandness, overcautious) and neutrality shift terms (hedged, vague, diluted).
  \item \textbf{Dual-Sampling Strategy}: Combine (i) enriched keyword sample and (ii) random baseline sample to enable precision/recall estimation for complaint detection heuristics via manual labels.
  \item \textbf{Annotation Batch Scaling}: Target initial labeled size \(n \approx 200\) (balanced across enriched vs baseline) to estimate label prevalence and adjust sampling weights.
  \item \textbf{Reliability Pass}: Introduce second annotator on a 15\% overlap slice; compute Cohen's \(\kappa\) and per-class agreement; refine ambiguous tag definitions (e.g., distinguish \emph{helpfulness\_regression} vs \emph{verbosity\_change}).
  \item \textbf{Preliminary Modeling}: Fit logistic regression predicting any complaint vs none using current stylistic features; inspect odds ratios for hedge rate, warmth rate, and verbosity proxies.
  \item \textbf{Drift Lexicon Seed}: Generate differential term list (enriched vs baseline) controlling for token length to initialize a persona drift candidate lexicon for later validation.
  \item \textbf{Ethical Guardrail Review}: Vet planned quoting policies and ensure de-identification and paraphrasing rules are codified prior to releasing intermediate examples.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Decision Log (Current Rationale)}
We defer implementing heavier topic modeling (e.g., BERTopic) until a minimally sufficient labeled base exists to evaluate whether added thematic clustering improves complaint taxonomy coverage or merely refines already captured lexical signals. Similarly, we postpone multi-model API probing until at least one authentic temporal Reddit slice confirms measurable drift dimensions to operationalize as controlled prompts.

\paragraph{Risk Mitigation} Key risks: (a) Over-fitting complaint detection to lexicon; (b) Absence of genuine pre window; (c) Annotator drift on subtle categories (helpfulness vs creativity). Mitigations: dual sampling + baseline; historical backfill; early reliability statistics with iterative guideline refinement.

\paragraph{Exit Criteria for Next Milestone} Proceed to API comparative probing only after: (i) \(\geq 150\) high-quality labeled Reddit instances with \(\kappa > 0.65\) overall; (ii) established top 10 drift candidate lexemes with effect size (log-odds) \(|z| > 2\) between authentic pre/post windows.

\subsection*{Robustness and Bias Mitigation (Added During Implementation)}
We integrated guardrails to ensure that any eventual persona drift claims reflect stable, non-artifactual patterns:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Sampling Bias Quantification}: The enrichment evaluation tool computes enriched precision vs baseline prevalence, relative risk, and a conservative recall estimate (Wilson CIs). We will report these values to justify that enrichment does not materially distort complaint incidence.
  \item \textbf{Reliability Gating}: Dual annotator splits with fixed overlap (default 25 items per 140-sample batch) enable scheduled computation of Cohen's \(\kappa\). Modeling of complaint shifts is skipped until \(\kappa > 0.65\) and minority class counts exceed 8 instances per binary contrast.
  \item \textbf{Model Separation Handling}: Logistic regressions auto-fallback to L2-penalized estimation when classical maximum likelihood fails (singular matrix / quasi-separation) and skip outcomes with insufficient class support (MIN\_CLASS=8) to avoid inflated odds ratios.
  \item \textbf{Lexical Drift Stability}: Drift computation applies a symmetric per-side frequency floor (default 10) and can cache raw counts for reproducibility; tokens below the floor are excluded to mitigate noisy one-sided spikes.
  \item \textbf{Temporal Authenticity}: A historical backfill scaffold normalizes authentic GPT-4 to GPT-4o window archives; artificial pre/post tags (from placeholder release anchors) are excluded from analysis once authentic windows are loaded.
  \item \textbf{Transparency of WIP}: Synthetic pipeline validation outputs (e.g., provisional regressions on randomized labels) are explicitly segregated and never interpreted as empirical findings.
\end{itemize}
These measures uphold the paper's goal: attributing observed user–system misalignment to genuine mutual wanting tension rather than artifacts of sampling, labeling noise, or early sparse data.
