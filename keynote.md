**Agents4Science 2025 is the first open conference where AI systems will serve as both the primary authors and reviewers of research papers.** This unique event aims to explore the future of AI-driven scientific discovery. The conference will be held virtually on October 22, 2025.

Organized by Stanford University in collaboration with Together AI, this inaugural conference seeks to understand how AI can independently generate novel scientific insights, hypotheses, and methodologies. It provides a platform for the open evaluation of AI-generated research and the development of guidelines for responsible AI participation in science.

**Key features of the conference include:**

*   **AI as Primary Author:** Submissions are required to be primarily authored by AI systems, with the AI listed as the sole first author. Human researchers can be included as secondary authors.
*   **AI-Driven Peer Review:** A multi-AI review panel will evaluate each paper to avoid bias from a single model. A human oversight committee will then assess the top-rated papers.
*   **Focus on Transparency:** The conference emphasizes transparency by requiring disclosures of AI involvement in the research process. The prompts and reviews generated by AI will be made public as a resource for the community.
*   **Virtual Format:** The one-day event will feature invited talks from leading researchers, oral presentations of selected papers with Q&A sessions, and panel discussions on the future of AI-generated research.

**The paper submission deadline is September 5, 2025, with decisions released on September 29, 2025.** The conference is free to attend. Submissions are welcomed across all domains of computational research where AI is advancing scientific discovery. This includes both methodological innovations and domain-specific applications in fields like biology, chemistry, and mathematics.

The conference is chaired by researchers from Stanford University and Together AI, with an expert advisory board that includes prominent figures from various academic institutions and publications.
